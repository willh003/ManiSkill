# Making vanilla PPO work on 4 fixed holes (shared peg), 2026-11-20

Setup: `ppo_tasks.py --fixed --shared_peg --n_tasks M`, PegInsertionSideBias-v1, 2048 envs, ppo_fast recipe (gamma .97, 8 epochs, 16 steps).
Tracker experiment: `ppo-4holes-fix`.

## Baselines (from scratch, 75M steps, final train success)
M=1: .99 (klone) | M=4: **0.00** (klone seed 1, Tillicum seed 2) | M=32: .96 | M=128: .73 (klone; Tillicum copy 0.00, suspicious).
M=4 never leaves return ~8 (entropy collapses, bad local optimum); M=32 contains the same 4 holes and works.

## Finding: warm start needs care
Warm start from `peg-expert-r1/ckpt_3576.pt` (random-task PPO expert) already gives train .95 / held-out .89 at step 0.
Plain `--checkpoint` at lr 3e-4 collapses to success 0 within 50 iterations: the expert's action std is tiny (logstd ~ -2.8), so the first
Adam steps give KL ~8, plus the critic is mismatched. Fix (commit 1b932a3): `--critic_warmup_iters 30` (actor lr 0, critic only) and `--actor_lr`.

## Runs (Tillicum, `logs/runs/<name>`, 20M steps warm / 60M scratch, seed 1)
- p4-warm-lr1e5, p4-warm-lr3e5: train 1.00 through 3.3M steps; held-out/fullspace drift down (specialising to 4 holes; faster at 3e-5: fullspace .88 -> .61).
- p4-scratch-ent003 / ent01 (entropy bonus): 0.00 through 26-27M steps -> cancelled. Entropy does not fix the plateau.
- **p4-scratch-nh25 / nh5 (OmniReset-style reset bias, `--near_hole_prob`)**: with prob p a train reset starts with the peg already in the hole, aligned,
  pulled back U(0.03,0.10) m along the hole axis (robot default pose; eval envs unbiased). From scratch, 4 holes, seed 1:
  nh25: train .16 @9M -> .85 @11.5M -> .96 @13.1M; nh5: .71 @9M, noisy .38-.79 @11-13M. First from-scratch recipe that works on 4 holes.
- Warm runs @16-17M: train 1.00 throughout, held-out .48/.19, fullspace .46/.17 (1e-5 / 3e-5): specialisation to the 4 holes.
## Result
**Warm-started PPO (finished, 20M steps): train success 1.00 at every eval from step 0 to 19.7M, both actor lrs** (held-out/fullspace at end: 1e-5 .41/.39,
3e-5 .33/.25; i.e. it specialises to the 4 holes). Checkpoints: `logs/runs/p4-warm-lr1e5/final_ckpt.pt`, `p4-warm-lr3e5/final_ckpt.pt` (also on Tillicum).
This meets the goal with pretrained init (peg-expert-r1 + critic warmup + low actor lr).
From scratch: reset bias (nh25 .98 @16M; nh5 noisier .30 @15.6M) still running to 60M (jobs 504060/504061); entropy bonus and plain PPO fail.
