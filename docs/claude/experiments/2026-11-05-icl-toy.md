# ICL toy: BC vs ICL with g-dropout p
Setup in [implementations/icl-toy.md](../implementations/icl-toy.md). N in {5,10,20,35,50}, 5 seeds, 3000 full-batch Adam steps. Outputs: logs/toy/toy.png, logs/toy/results.json (not committed; no ckpts saved).

Test (x<0) success (|err|<0.05) pass@1 -> pass@2, N=50: BC .07->.07; ICL p=0 .07->.07; p=.5 .09->.10; p=.75 .09->.10; p=1 .08->.20.
Test MSE pass@2 at N=50: BC .267, p=0 .243, p=.25 .226, p=.5 .141, p=.75 .161, p=1 .064.
Train pass@1 (x>0): BC/p<=.5 ~exact; p=.75 ~.98-1.0; p=1 only ~.1 success (MSE .1-.4) — crashes pass@0 as predicted.

Takeaways
- BC fails on x<0 (MSE ~.25) and ignores feedback; ICL p=0 behaves like BC (shortcut pi(x,g)).
- Test pass@2 improves monotonically with p (feedback use); train pass@1 degrades only at p near 1. p~0.5-0.75 is the balance: train unharmed, test pass@2 MSE about halved vs BC.
- Even p=1 only reaches ~20-29% test success at pass@2 (single correction step, tiny MLP, tol .05); not tuned.
